# Complaints Policy

## 1. Who can complain
Any registered user — store, supplier or representative. A complaint can reference an order, a return, or a party.

## 2. Categories
Product quality, delivery, pricing, representative conduct, commissions, account & technical, privacy, other.

## 3. Handling
1. The other party is notified and given a chance to respond.
2. Platform administrators follow up complaints not resolved between the parties.
3. Targets: first response within 2 business days; resolution or reasoned decision within 10 business days.
4. All replies and attachments are kept in the complaint record.

## 4. Outcomes
May include amicable settlement, a warning, temporary suspension, cancellation of an authorization, or removal of a non-compliant product or rating. **The platform cannot refund money** because it never holds it; financial settlement is between the parties.

## 5. Escalation
Users may request reconsideration and may also contact competent authorities such as the Ministry of Commerce.

## 6. Abusive complaints
Repeated false complaints intended to cause harm may lead to account restrictions.
