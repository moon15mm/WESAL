# Privacy Policy

> Draft requiring specialist legal review under the **Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)** and its implementing regulations before final adoption.

## 1. Data we collect
| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Name, mobile, email, language | Account creation and communication |
| Business data | CR, VAT number, addresses and branches | Verification and order fulfilment |
| Operational data | Orders, approvals, returns, commissions | Providing the service and records |
| Visit data | Visit time, location **only with your consent** | Documenting representative visits |
| Technical data | IP address, device type, session log | Security and fraud prevention |

## 2. Legal basis
Performance of contract, legal obligations, legitimate interest in platform security, and explicit consent for location and marketing messages.

## 3. Location
Location is captured only when a representative checks in/out of a visit **and grants permission on the device**. There is no background tracking. Consent can be withdrawn at any time in browser or device settings.

## 4. Who sees your data
- Each business sees only its own data and its own transactions; isolation is enforced at database level.
- A supplier sees the store data needed to fulfil its orders; a representative sees customers within their authorization.
- Service providers (hosting, email, SMS, AI when enabled) under contracts requiring confidentiality and data minimisation.
- Authorities when legally required.

## 5. Transfers outside the Kingdom
Data is hosted in the Kingdom by default. If a provider processes data abroad (e.g. an AI provider), this happens only under the PDPL transfer rules, and administrators can disable such services.

## 6. Retention
Data is kept for the periods in the **Data Retention Policy**, then deleted or anonymised.

## 7. Your rights
To be informed, to access, to obtain a copy, to correct, to request destruction (subject to legal obligations such as keeping financial records), and to withdraw consent — via **Settings → Privacy** or the contact form.

## 8. Security
Encrypted transport, Argon2 password hashing, encryption of sensitive fields, two-factor authentication, an append-only audit log, and encrypted backups.

## 9. Incident notification
If a breach affects your data we will notify the competent authority and affected users within the statutory deadlines.

## 10. Contact
For privacy questions use the Contact page and choose the "Privacy" topic.
