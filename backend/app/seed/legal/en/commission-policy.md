# Commission Policy

## 1. Source
Commission is defined in a **commission agreement** set by the supplier and linked to the authorization. The platform only calculates and records; it **does not pay or hold commissions**.

## 2. Rule types
- Percentage of order or line value.
- Fixed amount per unit.
- Fixed amount per order.
Rules can apply to all products, a category, a brand, a specific product, or new customers. **The most specific rule wins** when rules overlap.

## 3. Basis
Net value of delivered lines after discounts and before VAT, unless the agreement states otherwise.

## 4. Status lifecycle
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Estimated | When the order is approved |
| Pending | After delivery, during the hold period (covers returns) |
| Due | After the hold period ends |
| Approved | After supplier review |
| Paid | After the supplier records payout evidence |
| Disputed | When the representative disputes it |
| Adjusted | After a return or dispute settlement |
| Cancelled | When the order is cancelled or fully returned |

## 5. No double counting
At most one commission per representative per order, and each return adjustment is applied only once.

## 6. Returns
Commission on returned lines is deducted; if already paid, a negative adjustment is recorded and offset against later earnings.

## 7. Disputes
A representative may dispute a commission within 30 days of it becoming due, stating the reason; the supplier responds within 14 days.
