# Returns Policy

## 1. Principle
Returns are between the store and the supplier; the platform provides the request and documentation tools.

## 2. Reasons
Damage, short quantity, wrong item, expired or near-expiry on receipt, manufacturing defect, plus any extra cases in the supplier's own policy.

## 3. Time limits
- Shortage and visible damage: within **48 hours** of delivery.
- Quality and expiry issues: within **7 days** of delivery, unless the supplier offers longer.

## 4. Process
1. The store submits the return with items, quantities, reason and photos.
2. The supplier accepts or rejects with a reason within 3 business days.
3. If accepted, the parties agree on pickup, replacement or a credit note.
4. The order moves to "partially returned" or "fully returned", and commissions are adjusted automatically.

## 5. Money
**The platform neither receives nor refunds money;** the supplier issues a credit note or refunds the store directly.

## 6. Exclusions
Chilled and frozen goods not stored according to the storage conditions after receipt, and opened products without a defect.
