# Terms & Conditions

> **Operational draft** describing how the WESAL platform works. It must be reviewed by a lawyer licensed in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia before final adoption. Nothing here states that the platform holds any licence or official certification unless explicitly announced with its number.

## 1. Definitions
- **Platform / WESAL:** the online marketplace connecting stores with suppliers and sales representatives.
- **Store:** a registered retail business or supermarket buying for commercial purposes.
- **Supplier:** a company, brand or distributor that lists, sells and delivers its products.
- **Representative:** an independent or company sales representative working for a supplier under a valid **authorization**.
- **Authorization:** a recorded approval from a supplier defining the products, areas, commission and period a representative may work within.

## 2. The platform's role
1. WESAL is a technology intermediary for listing, ordering, communication and record-keeping.
2. **The platform does not own goods** or sell them in its own name; the sale contract is between the store and the supplier.
3. **The platform is not a courier;** the supplier delivers, directly or through its own contractors, at its own responsibility.
4. **The platform does not receive or hold purchase funds;** the store pays the supplier directly and the platform only records the collection status entered by the parties.

## 3. Registration & accounts
- Users must provide accurate, current information, including commercial registration and VAT number where applicable.
- The platform may request verification documents and suspend an account until they are provided.
- Users are responsible for their credentials; two-factor authentication is recommended and mandatory for administrators.
- A business may add team members with defined permissions and remains responsible for their actions.

## 4. Orders
1. **Direct order:** created by the store and sent to the supplier.
2. **Representative order:** prepared by a representative within their authorization; **it becomes binding only after the store explicitly approves** the presented version (items, quantities, prices, offers, delivery date).
3. Any material change by the supplier (price, quantity, substitute item) requires a new store approval.
4. An immutable snapshot of each order is stored at approval time as evidence.
5. Prices are set by the supplier; representatives cannot change prices beyond what the supplier allows in the system.

## 5. Prices & VAT
- Prices are shown in Saudi riyals (SAR) and indicate whether VAT is included.
- The supplier is responsible for pricing and for issuing a compliant tax invoice to the store under ZATCA requirements.

## 6. Delivery & receipt
- The supplier commits to the delivery dates in the order and updates the shipment status.
- The store checks the delivery on receipt and records any shortage or damage within the periods in the Returns Policy.

## 7. Commissions
Representative commissions are calculated under the commission agreement linked to the authorization and the Commission Policy; the supplier pays the representative directly and the platform records the payout evidence.

## 8. Acceptable use
Prohibited: impersonation, counterfeit or prohibited products, rating manipulation, attempts to access other businesses' data, circumventing regulations, and spam.

## 9. Liability
- Each party is responsible for its contractual obligations to the other.
- The platform does not guarantee product quality or party performance but provides documentation, complaint and rating tools.
- To the extent permitted by law, the platform's liability is limited to subscription fees paid in the three months before the event.

## 10. Suspension & termination
The platform may suspend or close an account for breaching these terms or applicable law, notifying the user of the reason; the user may appeal through the complaints system.

## 11. Governing law
These terms are governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, including the E-Commerce Law and the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL); Saudi courts have jurisdiction.

## 12. Changes
Changes are published with a new version number and notified to users a reasonable time before taking effect.
