# Ratings Policy

## 1. Who rates whom
- Stores rate suppliers and representatives after an order completes.
- Suppliers rate representatives.
- **Only parties to a real, recorded transaction may rate**, one rating per order.

## 2. Criteria
Quality, punctuality, order accuracy, communication, professionalism.

## 3. Prohibited content
Abuse, personal information, advertising, paid or reciprocal ratings, and rating a business the rater owns.

## 4. Replies & reports
The rated party may reply publicly and report a non-compliant rating. Administrators review reports and may hide a non-compliant rating while keeping it in the record.

## 5. No manipulation
Ratings are never changed for a fee, and genuine negative ratings are not removed.
