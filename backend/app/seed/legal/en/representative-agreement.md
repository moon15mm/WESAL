# Representative Agreement

> Draft requiring legal review, including how labour and freelance regulations apply to the representative–supplier relationship.

## 1. Relationship
- An independent representative works on their own account and may work with several companies, **provided they hold a valid, active authorization from each**.
- A company representative works within a registered business that manages their permissions.
- The platform is not the representative's employer and pays no wage or commission.

## 2. Authorization
1. The representative requests or receives an authorization; it takes effect only after they accept the presented terms.
2. The representative works **only within the authorized products, areas and period**; the system automatically rejects anything outside scope.
3. Expiry or suspension immediately blocks new orders for that supplier.

## 3. Orders
- The representative prepares the order and sends it to the store; **it is confirmed only by the store's explicit approval**.
- Representatives may not change prices, grant discounts the supplier did not set, or present an order as approved.
- Representatives may not collect money on behalf of the supplier unless authorized in writing by the supplier outside the platform, at both parties' responsibility.

## 4. Visits & location
Location capture is optional and requires consent at each check-in; there is no background tracking.

## 5. Commission
- Calculated under the commission agreement linked to the authorization and the Commission Policy.
- Moves through: estimated → pending → due → approved → paid, and may be adjusted for returns or cancellation.
- The supplier pays directly; the representative may dispute within the stated period.

## 6. Professional conduct
Honest product presentation, respect for the store's decision, no disparagement of competitors, and confidentiality of customer data.

## 7. Ratings
Stores and suppliers may rate the representative under the Ratings Policy; the representative may reply and dispute.
