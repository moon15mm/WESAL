# WESAL user guide

**One Market. Connected.**

WESAL connects stores and supermarkets with suppliers and sales representatives. The supplier lists products
and delivers; the store orders and **pays the supplier directly**; the representative visits stores and prepares
orders under an authorization from the company. The platform does not hold money or goods.

## Getting started

1. Choose your language with the globe icon: العربية, English, اردو, हिन्दी. The page direction switches automatically.
2. Click **Create account** and pick **Store**, **Company / supplier** or **Sales representative**.
3. Enter name, mobile, e-mail and a strong password; accept the terms.
4. Enter the verification codes sent to your e-mail and mobile.
5. Complete **Business profile**, upload your commercial registration and click **Submit for verification**.
   The WESAL team reviews the documents and notifies you.

Turn on **Two-factor verification** in **Settings → Security** (mandatory for platform administrators) and store
your recovery codes safely.

## Stores

| Screen | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Purchases summary, orders waiting for your decision, top suppliers, reorder suggestions |
| **Marketplace** | Search by name, brand or barcode, compare suppliers' offers for the same product, choose piece or carton and quantity, **Add** |
| **Cart** | One cart per supplier; review totals, VAT and delivery fee, then place the order |
| **Approvals** | Orders prepared by representatives, price offers and supplier changes — nothing is confirmed without you |
| **Orders** | Track each order: New → Approved → Preparing → On the way → Delivered → Completed |
| **Smart reorder** | Suggestions based on your purchase history |
| **Returns** | Request a return for damaged or missing items with photos; follow the supplier's decision |
| **Branches** / **Team** | Add branches; invite staff as branch manager, purchasing officer, accountant or receiving staff |

### Approving an order prepared by a representative

1. You get a notification; the order appears in **Approvals** as *Awaiting store approval*.
2. Open it and check items, quantities, prices, offers and delivery date.
3. Click **Approve order** — exactly what you saw is recorded, and only then does the supplier receive it.
4. Or **Request changes** / reject it with a reason.

If the supplier later changes a price or quantity, the order comes back to you for a new approval.

### Receiving and paying

When the delivery arrives, open the order and click **Confirm receipt**, recording any shortage or damage per
item. Pay **the supplier directly** as agreed; the supplier records the collection evidence.

## Representatives

| Screen | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Today's visits, sales attributed to you, commissions due, tasks, authorization status |
| **Companies** | Discover companies and request an authorization |
| **Authorizations** | Read the terms (products, cities, commission, period), tick *I have read and accept these terms and the commission agreement* and click **Accept authorization** |
| **Visits** | Plan a visit or start one now; location is captured only with your consent at check-in and check-out |
| **Customers** / **Discover stores** | Your customer base and new stores in your areas |
| **Catalog** | Only the products of companies you are authorized for, at the supplier's prices |
| **Orders → New** | Choose store and company, add items (or type the order in **Quick entry**), then **Send to store for approval** |
| **Commissions** | Expected → Pending (hold period) → Due → Approved → Paid; **Dispute** with a reason if something is wrong |
| **My profile** | Experience, languages, areas, categories and visibility in the representatives marketplace |

You cannot change prices, order outside your authorization, or finalise an order without the store's approval.

## Suppliers / companies

| Screen | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Products** | Add products (or import a CSV file): piece and carton price, minimum quantity, availability, stock, image |
| **Prices & offers** | Price lists for specific customers or regions; percentage, fixed or quantity-tier promotions with dates |
| **Coverage** | Cities you deliver to, delivery fee and lead time |
| **Orders** | **Accept order**, or **Send price offer** with changed prices/quantities (the store must approve it), then Prepare → Ready → Dispatch with driver → Delivered with proof |
| **Representatives** / **Authorizations** | Review requests, issue authorizations with products, cities, commission agreement and dates, suspend or cancel with a reason, add your own employee representatives |
| **Commissions** | Agreements and rules, approve due commissions, record payouts with evidence, resolve disputes |
| **Customers** | Each customer's file, acquisition source, history, notes and contacts |
| **Tasks & targets** | Tasks for representatives and sales targets |
| **Returns** | Approve or reject returns, record receipt and settlement |
| **Reports** | Sales, products, representatives and customers, with export |

## Reports & export

Every report exports to Excel, CSV or PDF in the **Documents language** chosen in **Settings** (it can differ
from the interface language), with correct right-to-left layout for Arabic and Urdu.

## AI assistant

Turns text like "5 cartons water, 3 milk" into suggested items and answers questions such as "How much did I buy
this month?" from your own data only. It never invents prices, approves orders or changes data.

## Notifications & privacy

* **Settings → Notifications**: choose channels per notification type.
* **Settings → Privacy**: download a copy of your data or request account deletion.
* **Settings → Active sessions**: review signed-in devices and end any session.

## Complaints & ratings

After an order completes you can rate the supplier and the representative. For any problem open
**Complaints → New complaint** linked to the order; the other party replies and the WESAL team follows up.

## Platform administration

**Verification** (businesses and representatives), **Users**, **Orders**, **Commissions** and **Complaints**
oversight, **Catalog** and **Ratings** moderation, **Subscriptions**, **Advertising**, **Content pages** (legal
texts), **Languages**, **Settings**, **Audit log** and **System health**.
