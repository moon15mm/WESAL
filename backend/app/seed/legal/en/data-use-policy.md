# Data Use Policy

## 1. Principle
Data is used **only for the purpose it was collected for**, and only as much as needed.

## 2. Permitted uses
- Fulfilling orders, approvals, deliveries and returns.
- Calculating commissions and producing each business's reports about its own data.
- Security, fraud prevention and audit logs.
- Aggregated, non-identifying statistics to improve the platform.

## 3. Prohibited uses
- Selling personal data to anyone.
- One business viewing another business's data beyond their mutual transactions.
- Using store data to target stores with competing offers outside the platform without consent.

## 4. AI assistant
- Works only within the user's permissions and their business's data.
- Never invents prices, approves orders or changes data.
- When an external provider is used, the minimum data is sent; administrators can disable the assistant entirely.

## 5. Reports & exports
Exports are available only to authorized users within their own business, and every export is written to the audit log.
